Model. Lattice Boltzmann simulation on a 2 cm grid (12 million cells), air at 1.23 kg/m³ (600 m, −5 °C), shown here on a 4 cm grid; feed lines traced on the full 2 cm grid. The fan is regulated to each target airflow; the pressure it needs shows how much the surroundings resist.

  • Fan modelled as an even push, coil as an even resistance; louvre slats, wind and cold outlet air sinking are left out.
  • The 6 cm gap under the unit is three grid cells.
  • All treads on and treads 7–13 removed: four fan levels, interpolated in between. Whole stair removed: about 2,200 m³/h only, scaled for other levels.
  • It shows how air reaches the unit. It cannot reproduce the 67 Hz drone, which forms inside the unit.
